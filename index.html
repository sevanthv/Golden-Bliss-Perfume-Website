THE ART OF PERFUMERY

The Olfactory Pyramid

A luxury perfume unfolds over time like a musical composition. Learn how each tier reveals its character.

🍋
First 15 - 30 Minutes

Top Notes

The luminous first impression that greets your senses. Typically vibrant notes of Italian Bergamot, Pink Pepper, Citruses, and Crisp Pear.

🌹
2 - 6 Hours

Heart Notes

The true soul and character of the fragrance that emerges as top notes fade. Features Damask Rose, French Vanilla, Peony, and Royal Jasmine.

🌲
8 - 14+ Hours

Base Notes

The enduring foundation that lingers on your skin and clothes for days. Rich Cambodian Oud, Warm Golden Amber, Sandalwood, and Tonka Bean.
